Child Safety Standards & CSAE Prevention Policy

Published & Effective: November 2026 | Operator: Sebastian Holzapfel

1. Zero Tolerance for Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE)

ThaiVibe maintains an absolute, uncompromising zero-tolerance policy towards child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and child sexual exploitation and abuse (CSAE) in any form. We are dedicated to providing a safe, strictly adult dating environment and preventing our platform from ever being used to exploit, harm, or endanger minors.

2. Strict 18+ Age Restriction

ThaiVibe is exclusively designed and operated for adults aged 18 and older:

  • Minors under the age of 18 are strictly prohibited from creating accounts, browsing, or using any part of the service.
  • Users must provide their birthdate upon registration. Any user attempting to create an account under age 18 is immediately blocked.
  • Any discovered or reported profile belonging to or depicting a minor is permanently deleted immediately.

3. Prohibited Content and Conduct

The following are strictly banned from ThaiVibe without exception:

  • Any visual or textual depictions of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).
  • Grooming, solicitation, or sexual conversations directed at or involving minors.
  • Sharing, transmitting, linking to, or requesting child exploitation or trafficking material.
  • Any profile photos depicting minors, even if incidental or accompanied by adults.

4. In-App Reporting & Detection Mechanisms

ThaiVibe provides immediate, robust tools for users to flag and report any safety concerns:

  • Direct In-App Profile & Chat Reporting: Every profile card, detail screen, and chat room includes easily accessible safety and reporting buttons. Users can instantly flag content or suspicious behavior with predefined categories including underage suspicion and inappropriate content.
  • Instant Blocking: Users can block abusive or suspicious individuals with a single tap, severing all communication channels immediately.
  • Content Moderation: Reported profiles and flagged messages are prioritized for rapid review and action.

5. Law Enforcement Cooperation & Mandatory Reporting

In full compliance with regional, national, and international child safety laws:

  • Upon identifying confirmed or suspected child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or child exploitation, ThaiVibe immediately terminates the offender's account.
  • We preserve relevant evidentiary records in accordance with legal requirements and promptly report instances to designated law enforcement authorities and recognized reporting clearinghouses, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and regional cybercrime units.
  • We actively cooperate with authorized law enforcement investigations into crimes involving child safety.
