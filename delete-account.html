User Data Control

Account & Data Deletion

In compliance with Google Play Data Safety policies, this page explains how to delete your ThaiVibe account and what data is removed.

1 Direct Deletion inside the ThaiVibe App (Recommended)

If you have the ThaiVibe app installed on your phone, you can instantly delete your entire profile without waiting for manual verification:

  1. Log in to the account you wish to delete in the ThaiVibe app.
  2. Tap on Profile in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Tap the Settings icon (gear ⚙️) in the top-right corner.
  4. Scroll all the way down and tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion prompt. Your account and personal data will be wiped permanently.

2 Web Request (If app is already uninstalled)

To prevent unauthorized deletion of stranger accounts, requests sent via web or email require proof of ownership. We verify this by sending a one-time confirmation code or email verification link to the registered email address before executing the deletion.

How to submit a deletion request:

Send an email to our support team from the email address registered with your ThaiVibe account:

To: support@thaivibe.app
Subject: ThaiVibe Account Deletion Request
Details needed: Your registered email address and display name.
✉️ Open Pre-filled Deletion Email

What data is deleted and what is retained?

Permanently Deleted:

  • User profile, bio, age, preferences
  • Profile photos & media uploads
  • GPS and location history data
  • Chat messages & conversations
  • Push notification tokens (FCM)

Retained (Limited Time):

  • Customer support emails (retained up to 90 days for dispute resolution)
  • Aggregated, fully anonymized metrics (not linked to your identity)
  • Records required by applicable legal, fraud-prevention, or regulatory statutes

Account deletion requests submitted by email are processed and purged within 7 business days following identity confirmation.
