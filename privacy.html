Privacy Policy for ThaiVibe

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction & Responsible Party

Welcome to ThaiVibe. We respect your personal data and privacy. This Privacy Policy informs you about how we process your personal data when you use the ThaiVibe mobile application and our services.

Controller / Operator:

Contact & Data Privacy Inquiries: support@thaivibe.app

2. Information We Collect

To provide dating and matchmaking services, we collect and process the following categories of data:

  • Profile & Account Information: Your email address, phone number (if used for authentication), display name, date of birth / age, gender, dating preferences, biography, and profile photos.
  • Location Information (GPS & Approximation): With your explicit permission, we access your device's geographical location (GPS coordinates). We process this data to show distance between users (e.g., "5 km away"). We do not disclose your exact geographic coordinates to other users; only approximate distance is displayed.
  • Messages & Communications: Chats, timestamps, and media you send to other users within the app.
  • Device & Technical Data: IP address, device type, operating system version, push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging), crash reports, and app performance logs.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your information strictly for the following purposes:

  • Creating, managing, and authenticating your user account.
  • Finding and displaying nearby singles based on your location preferences.
  • Delivering real-time chat messages and sending push notifications for matches and new messages.
  • Enforcing community safety, preventing spam, fake accounts, harassment, and unauthorized commercial activity.
  • Complying with legal obligations and Google Play Developer Policies.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We utilize trusted cloud infrastructure and service providers necessary to operate the application:

  • Supabase (Backend, Database & Authentication): Stores your user profile, messages, and encrypted credentials securely in modern cloud databases.
  • Google Firebase (Firebase Cloud Messaging - FCM): Processes device notification tokens to deliver instant push notifications when you receive a message or match.
  • Hosting & Content Delivery: We host our web infrastructure securely on Vercel.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your information as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated personal data directly within the ThaiVibe mobile app settings or via our dedicated web request portal.

Account & Data Deletion Request (Google Play Policy Compliance):

If you have uninstalled the app or wish to request immediate permanent deletion of your profile, messages, images, and stored data, please visit our Account Deletion Page or email support@thaivibe.app.

6. Age Restriction (18+)

ThaiVibe is strictly intended for individuals who are at least 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18. If we discover an account registered by someone under 18, it will be deleted immediately.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Under applicable privacy laws (such as GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request rectification of inaccurate data.
  • Request erasure (deletion) of your personal data.
  • Withdraw your consent to location tracking or push notifications at any time via your device settings.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
